Which UTM zone fits each Egyptian governorate?

Each governorate is coloured by the UTM zone utm-zone-finder returns for its bounding-box centre; dashed outlines span more than one zone. Click a governorate for its zone, EPSG code and spanned zones. Source and method: utm-zone-finder on GitHub.
